# Hey, I'm Pratish

Electrical engineer turned data engineer, based in Umhlanga, South Africa.

I've always liked taking a messy problem and turning it into something that just works. I started out doing that on railway infrastructure at Transnet, replacing catenary wire and chasing down substation failures. These days I do it with data: ETL pipelines, SQL Server warehouses and a lot of Python. Somewhere in between I picked up a habit of building weird hardware and entering hackathons.

## What I'm up to

- **Day job:** BI / Data Engineer at Tevo (since June 2023). I design and maintain ETL/ELT pipelines, and tune the SQL views, triggers and stored procedures that sit on top of them.
- **Studying:** Postgraduate Diploma in Data Science at UKZN, with a focus on forecasting and predictive modelling.
- **On the side:** running my own VPS with Docker, Linux and KVM/Libvirt, and building small tools and prototypes. My portfolio lives at [kyro.ninja](https://kyro.ninja).

## What I do at Tevo

- **Pipelines:** building data warehouse and backend solutions on Microsoft SQL Server and Azure. Ingestion, transformation, validation, making sure the numbers can be trusted.
- **Automation:** replacing manual reporting with Python, Pandas and a few custom apps. If I'm doing something twice, I'm already writing the script.
- **Deployment:** Flask apps on NGINX with SSL and virtual hosts, plus looking after the Azure VMs and services behind them.
- **Working with people:** gathering requirements from other teams, turning them into something useful, and documenting the BI/ETL processes so the next person isn't lost.

## Where I came from

Before data I spent about 5 years at Transnet, first as an electrical engineer (2018 to 2022) and then as Maintenance Manager: Planning (2022 to 2023). I looked after substation reliability, electrical asset lifecycles, OPEX/COPEX budgets and shutdown planning, and led a few teams along the way. It taught me a lot about reading data to find the real cause of a problem, which is basically the job I do now.

Some projects I'm proud of:

- **Boughton substation (2020 to 2021):** used Lean Six Sigma DMAIC to cut 3kV traction substation failures from about 1.1 a week to near zero. The root cause was a failing PCB component.
- **Estcourt to Rushbrook (2019 to 2020):** brought average weekly OHTE failures down from 1.13 to 0.51.
- **Ladysmith lightning trips (2021):** dug through five financial years of trip data, inspected the earthing at the substations and found incorrectly grounded arrestors. Redesigned the earthing and the trips dropped significantly.
- **Wire replacements:** 6 km of copper catenary swapped for aluminium (Glencoe to Uithoek, to stop theft), 7 km of contact wire in the Cedara Tunnel, and 20 km of rust-prone steel earth wire (Dundee to Tayside).
- **Performance report generator (2021):** an Excel VBA tool hooked to a live database that builds the 20-page monthly report and the PowerPoint in one click. Hours down to minutes. This is honestly where my automation habit started.
- **SAP scheduling macros:** automated maintenance scheduling in SAP with VBA, which took a pile of manual work off the department.

## Hardware projects

- **Custom in-car HUD (2019):** a Raspberry Pi with a 7" LCD in my Mahindra Scorpio, pulling live data from the OBD-II port. I also added a mode for passengers to switch over to retro games.
- **Mini Cooper scale model control system (2019):** the old model had no speed controller, so I built one with an Arduino Nano and an Xbox controller. Analog triggers handle acceleration, braking and direction, and there's an overdrive mode for short bursts of extra speed.
